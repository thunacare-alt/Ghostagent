What he cannot do

Most agent demos stop before this section. Ours does not — you should know the edges before you pay.

He is not a lawyer or an accountant

He drafts, extracts and reminds. A human signs off on anything with legal or statutory weight.

He cannot fix your data

If two spreadsheets disagree, he will tell you exactly where. He will not silently pick one.

He needs your numbers to be real

Quotes come from your rate card. If the rate card is stale, his quotes are stale.

He will ask before money moves

Payments, refunds and mass messages need an approval until you turn that off.

He is not on your payroll software

He is a worker, not a statutory employer record. Salary processing stays where it is.

WhatsApp can still rate-limit

He paces messages and respects the platform. Bulk blasting is exactly what we refuse to do.

Questions people actually ask

Is this just ChatGPT with a new skin?

No. ChatGPT answers; GHOST acts. He holds the tools — WhatsApp, your Sheets, your Drive, your site repo — and he runs on a schedule. The demo above is a real live call, not a video.

Where does my data live?

Your agent runs in its own isolated container with its own credentials. One customer's agent cannot read another's data, and your Google account is connected by you, revocable by you, at any time.

What happens if he gets something wrong?

Approvals stay on for the jobs that matter until you trust him. Every action is logged, and you can read the log from the panel — what he read, what he wrote, and when.

Can two people use the same agent?

Yes — five users are included. Staff, family or partners can each talk to him, and he answers according to who is asking.

What if I stop paying?

He stops. You keep your data — it lives in your Google account and your WhatsApp, not in ours.
