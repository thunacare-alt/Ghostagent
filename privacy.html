We do not sell your data and we do not use your workspace content to train models.

Who processes it

  • Infrastructure: the servers on which your workspace runs (our data-centre provider).
  • Email: our transactional email provider, used to send credentials, invoices and notices.
  • Model providers: prompts you send to an agent are forwarded to the model provider configured for your workspace (for example OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek or a local model). Their handling is governed by their terms; if you select a local model, no data leaves the box.
  • Messaging channels: if you connect Telegram, WhatsApp, Gmail or Slack, messages pass through those providers under their own policies.

Your controls

  1. Access and correction: ask us and we will correct account details.
  2. Export: ask for a full export at any time — you receive a verified archive of your workspace database, agents, memory and configuration before anything is deleted.
  3. Deletion: ask us to close the account. We take a final export, hand it to you (or hold it on your instruction), then erase the workspace and its agents. Some records (invoices) are retained because tax law requires it.
  4. Marketing: we only email you about your service and its billing.

Security

Passwords are generated per workspace and delivered only to the address on the order. Payment screenshots are stored on the server and served only to an authenticated operator holding the console token. Backups and exports are integrity-checked. If a breach affects your data we will tell you without undue delay.

Children

The service is sold to businesses and is not directed at anyone under 18.

Your rights under the DPDP Act

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask us to confirm what we hold, correct it, erase it, and nominate someone to act for you. Write to the grievance officer below; we resolve requests within 30 days and acknowledge within 48 hours of a complaint.

Cookies

This site sets no advertising or third-party tracking cookies. The checkout page is operated by our payment processor under its own policy.
